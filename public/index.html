♥ Portland hearts

Red paper hearts appear in Portland’s shop windows and on landmarks around Valentine’s Day. Volunteers carry on the tradition made famous by Kevin Fahrman, the Valentine Bandit.

Portland Hearts of Pine, Maine’s first professional soccer club, plays at Fitzpatrick Stadium. The club’s Dirigo Heart draws on the same Portland tradition.

About the listings

OpenStreetMap supplies community-mapped places and trails. Portland Museum of Art supplies events for the next 30 days. Yelp and Ticketmaster add listings when connected.

For more local events, browse the Portland Downtown events calendar.

Sample locations are labeled. Distances are straight-line miles from downtown Portland, not travel distances. Trail pins mark mapped locations.
